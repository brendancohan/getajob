## 🚫 Hard Disqualifiers / Automatic Rejection Criteria
The automated AI triage agent will filter out opportunities matching these rules:
* **Seniority Ceiling:** Roles demanding 10–15+ years of specialized executive pedigree (e.g., Director, VP, Vice President, Principal, Staff, Head of, Chief).
* **Unqualified Silos:** Roles outside your capability domain (e.g., non-relevant engineering specialties, sales quotas, entry-level internships).
* **Mandatory Clearances:** Positions requiring active security clearances (e.g., Top Secret, Secret, TS/SCI, Polygraph) if you do not hold one.
* **Licensing Barriers:** Positions requiring specialized state licenses you do not hold (e.g., PE license, RN, MD).

## 📝 Stylistic & Formatting Guidelines (ATS Takeaways)
All generated resumes must follow these rules for maximum applicant tracking system (ATS) and human recruiter impact:
* **Retain Hard Metrics:** Never replace quantifiable achievements with vague corporate fluff (e.g., always keep "$200/hr saved", "40% latency reduction", "100+ endpoints unified").
* **Years of Experience Upfront:** The Professional Summary must state years of experience in the very first sentence.
* **Authoritative Action Verbs:** Use strong leadership verbs (e.g., *"Led architecture and migration efforts"*, *"Engineered custom gateway pipelines"*).
* **Bold Skim-Headers:** Every bullet point must begin with a **Bold Category Header:** (e.g., **Infrastructure Automation:**, **Interface Engineering:**) to allow recruiters to scan core competencies in under 6 seconds.
* **Dedicated Key Projects Section:** Maintain a distinct *Key Projects & Technical Deliverables* section to substantiate your senior compensation bracket.
